# MetabolicMD™ USA | Clinical Weight Loss Healthcare Network

A clinical weight care web platform designed for individuals struggling to lose weight, featuring **100% Free Initial Doctor Consultations**, **Top US Board-Certified Physicians**, **Prestigious US Hospital Affiliations**, and **Zero Pricing**.

Live Production: [https://doctorplus-pi.vercel.app/](https://doctorplus-pi.vercel.app/)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **100% Free Consultation Guarantee (Zero Pricing)**:
   - No pricing plans, no subscription charges, no hidden credit card requirements.
   - Built for maximum lead conversion and email marketing collection.

2. **Top US Physicians & Hospital Network**:
   - **Dr. Sarah Jenkins, MD, FOMA** (Obesity Medicine & Endocrinology, *Cleveland Clinic Affiliate*)
   - **Dr. Marcus Vance, MD, PhD** (Bariatric Medicine & Metabolic Biology, *Johns Hopkins Medicine Affiliate*)
   - **Dr. Elena Rostova, MD** (Endocrinologist & GLP-1 Metabolic Specialist, *Mayo Clinic Affiliate*)
   - **Dr. David Chen, MD, MPH** (Preventive Cardiology & Metabolism, *Cedars-Sinai Affiliate*)
   - Affiliated institutions include *Mayo Clinic*, *Cleveland Clinic*, *Johns Hopkins Medicine*, *Cedars-Sinai*, *Mount Sinai*, and *Brigham and Women's Hospital*.

3. **High-Converting Lead Generation & Strict Verification**:
   - **Hero Quick Appointment Form**: Captures Name, Email, Phone, State, and Primary Struggle in under 30 seconds.
   - **Multi-Step Free Consultation Wizard**: An interactive 3-step modal collecting clinical profile, weight targets, and scheduling preferences.
   - **Strict Validation Guardrails**: RFC 5322 email verification, 10-digit phone verification with real-time formatting `(XXX) XXX-XXXX`, and error feedback preventing invalid submissions.
   - **Interactive BMI & Metabolic Resistance Calculator**: Calculates metabolic resistance scores and funnels into physician reviews.
   - **Free 2026 Clinical Protocol Guide (Lead Magnet)**: Secondary email capture for downloadable medical guide.

4. **Built-in Leads Marketing Hub & Email Exporter**:
   - Instant 1-click **"Copy All Emails"** for email marketing campaigns (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, ConvertKit, etc.).
   - Instant 1-click **"Export to CSV"** formatted for CRM and marketing software imports.
   - Accessible via the top announcement bar or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd>.
